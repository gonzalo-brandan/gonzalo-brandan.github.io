**About Me**

Hi, I’m a trilingual networking professional based in Berlin, working toward a career in network administration and infrastructure. I write about enterprise networking, Linux systems, and the hands-on lab work that helps turn theory into practical skill.

My main focus areas are Cisco routing and switching, network security, VPN technologies, and Ubuntu/Linux administration. I’m currently building my skills through labs, documentation, and real-world troubleshooting, with a strong interest in CCNP-level topics, automation, and modern network design.

This blog is where I share what I’m learning, what I’m building, and the lessons I pick up along the way. My goal is to make technical topics clear, useful, and practical for anyone studying networking or working in IT.
