[ Where I'm going ]

Building the frontier, in Europe

Technology keeps solving problems we thought were impossible a few years ago. It makes us better, smarter and faster, and it pushes our own limits. Right now that frontier is AI, and I honestly think we haven't seen its full potential yet. AI runs on hardware, networks and scale, and that's exactly the part I want to work on. And if it helps build Europe's digital sovereignty, even better.

[ What I enjoy · How I work ]

Hands on the hardware, in it for the long run

I'd rather spend my day on the floor than at a desk: walking through a facility, talking to the people who run it, and working out how all the hardware fits together through the network. I have a soft spot for a well-dressed rack, which is partly why there's one on this page. Most of what I know comes from reading and from trying things out in my homelab, and I enjoy the problems that take a while to figure out.

[ What I care about ]

Technology for people

Outside of work, I care about the things that affect all of us: an open society, open source, a world where everyone can be who they are and love who they love, and respect for people and the choices they make. I value privacy, and I'd like to see a world that keeps getting better for everyone, not just for a few.

Technology is only as fair as the people who build it.

[ What I'm looking for ]
